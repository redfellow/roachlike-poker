# Design
Replace `useMusicLevel` with a `useSoundLevel` preference (0–100, key `torakka:sound-volume`, default 100, legacy `torakka:muted=true` → 0). Pass a `level` instead of `muted` into `Session`/`Table`; every `playNormalizedAudio(src, v)` call becomes `playNormalizedAudio(src, v * level / 100)` and is skipped when level is 0. Delete `musicPlayer`, `useBackgroundMusic`, `BackgroundMusic`, the music constants, the switch markup and `.sound-toggle` CSS; rename `MusicVolume`/`.music-volume` to `SoundVolume`/`.sound-volume` and render it unconditionally in both topbars. A pure helper `soundVolume(base, level)` in `audio.ts` is unit-tested. Removing the switch also removes the Opera-specific failure since there is no checkbox switch left.
