# Design
Replace the `torakka:muted` switch preference with a `useSoundLevel` preference (0–100, key `torakka:sound-volume`, default 100, legacy `torakka:muted=true` → 0). Pass a `level` instead of `muted` into `Session`/`Table`; every `playNormalizedAudio(src, v)` call becomes `playNormalizedAudio(src, v * level / 100)` and is skipped when level is 0. Delete the switch markup and `.sound-toggle` CSS; add a `SoundVolume` slider (`.sound-volume`) and render it unconditionally in both topbars. A pure helper `soundVolume(base, level)` in `audio.ts` is unit-tested. Removing the switch also removes the Opera-specific failure since there is no checkbox switch left.
